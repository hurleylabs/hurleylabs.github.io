LinkScout Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Publisher: Hurley Labs

Summary

LinkScout: Broken Link Checker does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data or browsing data. Everything happens inside your browser.

What LinkScout does with your data

  • When you press "Check this page", LinkScout reads the links (addresses and link text) on the page you are viewing.
  • It then sends a request to each of those links, only to read the HTTP status (for example 200 or 404). These requests go directly from your browser to the websites the links point to, the same sites you would contact by clicking the links. They are sent without cookies or login information.
  • The results (URL, status, link text) are shown in the popup and outlined on the page. A copy for the current tab is kept in your browser's temporary session storage so the popup can show it again. It is deleted when you close the tab, navigate away, or close the browser.
  • If you press "Export CSV", the file is created on your device and saved by your browser. It is not uploaded anywhere.

What LinkScout does not do

  • No data is sent to Hurley Labs or to any third party.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or fingerprinting.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No remote code. All code ships inside the extension package.
  • No reading of page content other than the links, and nothing is read until you press "Check this page".

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: read the links on the current tab and outline them on the page.
  • storage: temporary per-tab session storage of the latest results.
  • Optional access to all sites: requested only when you press "Check this page", so the extension can contact the websites your links point to. You can remove it at any time in chrome://extensions.

Children

LinkScout is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the extension update and the effective date above will change.
