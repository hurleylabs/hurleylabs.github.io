ContrastScout Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

ContrastScout is a browser extension made by Hurley Labs.

Summary

ContrastScout does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data or browsing data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no remote servers.

What the extension does with your data

  • Page scanning. When you press "Scan this page", the extension reads the colors, font sizes and text of the visible elements on the current tab to calculate contrast. This happens inside your browser. The results are shown in the popup and are discarded when you close it or reload the page. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
  • Picked colors. The colors you pick or type are used only to calculate contrast. The last two colors are remembered in the extension popup's local storage on your device so they are still there next time. They never leave your device and you can remove them by clearing the extension's site data.
  • Eyedropper. The eyedropper is the browser's built-in feature. It returns only the single color you click.

Network access

ContrastScout makes no network requests. It loads no remote code, fonts, images or scripts.

Permissions

  • activeTab: temporary access to the current tab, granted only when you click the extension button.
  • scripting: runs the scan on that tab and draws or removes the outlines.

The extension requests no host permissions, so it cannot read pages in the background.

Children

The extension collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the extension and the date above will change.
